\section{Advanced Character Options}\label{sec:character-advanced}
This section describes Advanced Character Options, these are features and abilities that a character can gain access to as they advance.
Some of these options include narrative components that the player must answer when taking the option and may require GM approval as well.
This section is divided into several kinds of advanced options: Combat Styles, Attack Options, and Actions.
For advanced combat styles, the costs listed below is the cost to unlock the style, to actually get access to the style has additional XP costs as usual.

\subsection{Offensive Combat Styles}
\paragraph{Assassin}
You can only use this style to make an attack against a creature that is not aware of your presence.
When you do, you automatically succeed at the attack.
The range of attacks with this style is \emph{in point-blank range}.
Attacks with this style deal 1d8 damage.
\\[.5cm]
\underline{Cost}: 5 XP
\\
\underline{Upgrades:}
\begin{itemize}
  \item[-] The damage die can be changed to a d10 for 5 XP.
  \item[-] The range can be changed to \emph{within a short distance} for 3 XP.
  \item[-] When you use this style you can gain an Advantage that must be used to deal an additional die of damage for 4 XP.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Defensive Combat Styles}
\paragraph{Blink}
When you use this style, you can teleport a short distance to avoid an attack.
If the outcome on the roll to avoid a hit is Mixed, you avoid the damage entirely, if it is Bad you take half damage, and if it is Very Bad you still take full damage.
\\[.5cm]
\underline{Requires GM Approval}
\\
\underline{Cost}: 8 XP
\\
When you take this Defensive Combat Style, answer the following questions:
\begin{itemize}
  \item What is the place you travel through when you teleport?
  \item How do you avoid the madness that place could cause?
\end{itemize}
%
\underline{Upgrades:}
\begin{itemize}
  \item[-] Increase the distance you can teleport to a ``long distance'' for 5 XP.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Undying Fortitude}
When you use this style, if you take damage from a hit and the outcome of the roll was not Very Bad, your Life cannot be reduced below 1.
\\[.5cm]
\underline{Requires GM Approval}
\\
\underline{Cost}: 10 XP
\\
When you take this Defensive Combat Style, describe the source of your undying strength.

\subsection{Attack Options}
\paragraph{Wide Barrage}
When making an attack, you can spend an additional Main Action to target a group of opponents that are near each other.
\\[.5cm]
\underline{Cost}: 10 XP
\\
When you take this Attack Option, describe what your attack looks like.
\\
\underline{Upgrades:}
\begin{itemize}
  \item[-] Increase the distance the targeted opponents can be from each other to ``in the same vicinity'' for 10 XP.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Critical Attack}
When making an attack, you can spend an additional Main Action to double the damage you deal to the target.
\\
\underline{Cost}: 10 XP
\\
When you take this Attack Option, describe how you empower an attack.

\subsection{Alternative Main Actions}
\paragraph{Dedicated Healer}
When taking a Main Action to perform triage, you can spend a Defend Action to eliminate any Advantage cost required for that roll.
\\
\underline{Cost}: 8 XP
\\
\underline{Upgrades:}
\begin{itemize}
  \item[-] When you use this feature, you can also increase the roll to triage for 5 XP.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Transformation}
You can spend a Main Action and a Defend Action to transform your physical form; you can also transform once per session outside of combat.
Pick one of the following options for the remainder of the scene or until you end the transformation:
\begin{itemize}
  \item Your current and maximum Life increase by 10 for the duration (when you revert, if you still have any of these additional Life you lose them).
    While transformed, you cannot use any benefit of your Offensive Combat Styles other than range and the Basic Statistic's bonus to attack rolls, this means your damage is 0 plus any damage granted by any weapon you still wield.
  \item Whenever you deal damage while transformed add an additional damage die.
    While transformed, you cannot use any benefit of your Defensive Combat Styles other than the Basic Statistic's bonus to rolls to avoid being hit.
  \item Your speed is doubled and you gain a $+2$ bonus to all Finesse rolls for the duration.
    While transformed, you suffer a $-1$ penalty to your Strength and Willpower.
  \item You gain a $+2$ bonus to all Instinct rolls for the duration.
    Additionally, you gain any single Specialization, with a bonus of $+1$ and the Superhuman tag, with the ``Perceive'' verb; if you already have such a Specialization you can gain an additional $+1$ bonus to that Specialization instead.
    While transformed, you suffer a $-1$ penalty to your Presence and Knowledge.
\end{itemize}
Additionally, while transformed you cannot utilize any of your Specializations, other than any which receive a bonus by the transformation.
\\[.5cm]
\underline{Requires GM Approval}
\\
\underline{Cost}: 10 XP
\\
When you take this option, answer the following questions:
\begin{itemize}
  \item What do you look like when transformed?
  \item What instinct do you have to resist while transformed?
\end{itemize}
%
\underline{Upgrades:}
\begin{itemize}
  \item[-] The number of times you can transform out of combat can be increased to 5 for 5 XP or the limit can be removed entirely for 10 XP.
  \item[-] While transformed you can use any of your Specializations for 4 XP.
  \item[-] While transformed you can gain a Swim, Climb, or Fly (choose when you take this upgrade) speed equal to your Walk speed for 2 XP.
  \item[-] You can gain the ability to activate two options for 5 XP, the ability to activate three options for 12 XP, and the ability to activate all four options for 20 XP.
\end{itemize}
The four options additionally have the following upgrades for their positive benefits, each of which costs 5 XP:
\begin{itemize}
  \item Your current and maximum Life increase by 20 instead of 10.
  \item Whenever you deal damage add two additional damage dice, instead of one.
  \item Gain a $+4$, instead of $+2$, bonus to all Finesse rolls.
  \item Gain a $+4$, instead of $+2$, bonus to all Instinct rolls.
\end{itemize}
Additionally, the negative effects for a single option can be eliminated for 8 XP, or the effects for a single option can be reduced as described below for 4 XP:
\begin{itemize}
  \item You can benefit from the damage dice of your Offensive Combat Styles, though still do not benefit from any other bonuses or features they provide.
  \item While you are transformed you gain a $+1$ bonus to rolls to avoid being hit (though you still cannot use any benefits of your Defensive Combat Styles).
  \item When you transform you suffer a $-1$ penalty to only one of Strength and Willpower, you choose when you transform.
  \item When you transform you suffer a $-1$ penalty to only one of Presence and Knowledge, you choose when you transform.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Actions}
\paragraph{Additional Defend Actions}
You can increase the number of Defend Actions you receive each round for a number of XP equal to $8\times$ the number of Main Actions you will receive after the increase (so the first additional Main Action costs 16 XP, the next 24 XP).

\paragraph{Additional Main Actions}
You can increase the number of Main Actions you receive each round for a number of XP equal to $10\times$ the number of Main Actions you will receive after the increase (so the first additional Main Action costs 20 XP, the next 30 XP).

\paragraph{Action Carryover}
Instead of losing any Main or Defend Actions you have remaining at the end of a round of combat, you can keep 1 Main or Defend Action into the next round.
\\
\underline{Cost}: 5 XP
\\
\underline{Upgrades:}
\begin{itemize}
  \item[-] Increase the number of actions you can carry over to the next round for XP equal to $5 \times$ the new number of actions you can carry over.
\end{itemize}
